# HR Analytics Dashboard

##  Project Overview
- End-to-end HR Analytics project focused on employee workforce, attrition, hiring trends, and performance.
- Built using Excel/Power Query, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI.
- Designed to solve real-world HR business problems through data-driven analysis.

##  Business Objectives
- Analyze employee distribution across departments and job roles.
- Identify departments and roles with higher employee attrition.
- Understand factors associated with employee turnover.
- Analyze hiring trends over time and across departments.
- Evaluate employee performance across departments.
- Analyze the relationship between training and employee performance.

##  Tools & Technologies
- **Excel / Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **PostgreSQL** – Data storage and database management.
- **SQL** – Business analysis and data querying.
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and data visualization.

##  Key Analysis
- Total, active, and exited employees.
- Department-wise and job-role-wise employee analysis.
- Overall and department-wise attrition rate.
- Attrition by overtime, job satisfaction, and work-life balance.
- Hiring trends by year.
- Hiring analysis by department.
- Average salary by job role.
- Average performance rating by department.
- Training opportunities vs. employee performance.

##  Power BI Dashboard
- Interactive HR Analytics dashboard.
- KPI cards for workforce and attrition metrics.
- Department and job-role analysis.
- Attrition analysis using multiple employee factors.
- Hiring trend visualization.
- Employee performance and training analysis.
- Interactive filters for department, job role, gender, overtime, and business travel.

##  Business Value
- Helps HR identify areas with higher employee turnover.
- Supports investigation of potential attrition drivers.
- Provides visibility into workforce and hiring trends.
- Helps compare employee performance across departments.
- Supports data-driven HR decision-making.
