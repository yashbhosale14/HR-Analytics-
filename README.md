# HR-Analytics-
An end-to-end HR analytics project using Excel/Power Query, PostgreSQL, and Power BI to analyze employee attrition, hiring trends, workforce distribution, and performance. Built SQL-driven insights and an interactive dashboard to support data-driven HR decisions.

The project transforms raw HR data into SQL-driven business insights and an interactive Power BI dashboard to help HR teams identify attrition patterns, hiring trends, and performance opportunities.

Tools Used
Excel / Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
PostgreSQL – Data storage and SQL analysis
SQL – Business queries and insights
Power BI – Interactive dashboard and visualization

Key Analysis
Employee distribution by department and job role
Overall and department-wise attrition
Attrition analysis by overtime, job satisfaction, and work-life balance
Hiring trends over time
Hiring by department
Average salary by job role
Employee performance by department
Training opportunities vs. employee performance

Business Objective
To help HR teams understand where employees are leaving, potential factors associated with attrition, how hiring has changed over time, and how employee performance varies across the organization.
